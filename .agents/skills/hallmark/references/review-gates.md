# Final design review gates

Before finalizing a UI, check:

## Structure
- Can a user understand the page's purpose and primary action within a few seconds?
- Does the information order reflect user priority rather than template convention?
- Is there at least one meaningful structural decision specific to this product?

## Typography
- Are display and body sizes intentionally related?
- Are line lengths comfortable and hierarchy visible without relying on color?
- Are weights and tracking used sparingly rather than making everything bold?

## Surfaces
- Are cards used because information is actually grouped, not because cards are the default container?
- Are radius, border, and shadow values consistent and appropriate to the chosen visual register?
- Is there enough flat space to let important surfaces matter?

## Color
- Is there a clear neutral system and a disciplined accent strategy?
- Does color communicate hierarchy or state instead of merely decorating the background?
- Is contrast sufficient in light/dark and interactive states?

## Interaction
- Are hover, focus, disabled, loading, empty, error, and success states considered where relevant?
- Can keyboard and assistive-technology users operate the interface?
- Is motion purposeful and reduced-motion-safe?

## Anti-slop
- Remove decorative gradients, glows, pills, icons, badges, and cards that do not earn their place.
- Replace vague marketing copy with concrete product language.
- If the screen could belong to almost any AI startup after changing the logo, revise it.
